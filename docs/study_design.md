# Study Design

## Field Design
- 3 organic corn fields
- 3 conventional corn fields
- Nebraska region
- Fields located within approximately 60 miles of each other

## Sampling Schedule
- Pre-planting: days -7 to -1
- Mid-to-late growth: days 70-77
- 15-20 soil cores per field and time point
- Composite sampling
- Repeated across 3 growing seasons

## Data Types
- Metagenomic DNA
- Metatranscriptomic RNA
- Soil chemistry
- Weather and management metadata
- Corn yield

## Goal
Integrate microbial composition, gene expression, environmental variables, and field-scale yield to identify plant growth-promoting microbes and develop predictive models of soil health and crop productivity.
