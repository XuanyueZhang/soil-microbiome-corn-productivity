# Key References

This file highlights selected references that support the study design, sequencing workflow, bioinformatics pipeline, and predictive modeling plan for the Soil Microbiome and Corn Productivity project.

## Soil Microbiome and Sustainable Agriculture

- Sahoo A, Yadav G, Mehta T, Meena M, Swapnil P. Omics-driven insights into plant growth-promoting microorganisms for sustainable agriculture. *Discover Sustainability*. 2025.

- van Diepeningen AD, de Vos OJ, Korthals GW, van Bruggen AHC. Effects of organic versus conventional management on chemical and biological parameters in agricultural soils. *Applied Soil Ecology*. 2006.

- Tao J, Liu X, Liang Y, Niu J, Xiao Y, Gu Y, et al. Maize growth responses to soil microbes and soil properties after fertilization with different green manures. *Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology*. 2017.

## Metagenomics and Sequence Processing

- Thomas T, Gilbert J, Meyer F. Metagenomics — a guide from sampling to data analysis. *Microbial Informatics and Experimentation*. 2012.

- Chen S, Zhou Y, Chen Y, Gu J. fastp: an ultra-fast all-in-one FASTQ preprocessor. *Bioinformatics*. 2018.

- Nurk S, Meleshko D, Korobeynikov A, Pevzner PA. metaSPAdes: a new versatile metagenomic assembler. *Genome Research*. 2017.

- Kang DD, Li F, Kirton E, Thomas A, Egan R, An H, Wang Z. MetaBAT 2: an adaptive binning algorithm for robust and efficient genome reconstruction from metagenome assemblies. *PeerJ*. 2019.

## Metatranscriptomics

- Bushmanova E, Antipov D, Lapidus A, Prjibelski AD. rnaSPAdes: a de novo transcriptome assembler and its application to RNA-Seq data. *GigaScience*. 2019.

- Patro R, Duggal G, Love MI, Irizarry RA, Kingsford C. Salmon provides fast and bias-aware quantification of transcript expression. *Nature Methods*. 2017.

- Love MI, Huber W, Anders S. Moderated estimation of fold change and dispersion for RNA-seq data with DESeq2. *Genome Biology*. 2014.

## Multi-Omics and Predictive Modeling

- Rohart F, Gautier B, Singh A, Le Cao KA. mixOmics: an R package for 'omics feature selection and multiple data integration. *PLoS Computational Biology*. 2017.

- Chang HX, Haudenshield JS, Bowen CR, Hartman GL. Metagenome-wide association study and machine learning prediction of bulk soil microbiome and crop productivity. *Frontiers in Microbiology*. 2017.
