# Key References

This file highlights selected references that support the proposed sampling design, sequencing workflow, bioinformatics pipeline, and statistical analysis.

- van Diepeningen AD, de Vos OJ, Korthals GW, van Bruggen AHC. Effects of organic versus conventional management on chemical and biological parameters in agricultural soils. *Applied Soil Ecology*. 2006.
- Tao J, Liu X, Liang Y, et al. Maize growth responses to soil microbes and soil properties after fertilization with different green manures. *Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology*. 2017.

- Kang DD, Li F, Kirton E, et al. MetaBAT 2: an adaptive binning algorithm for robust and efficient genome reconstruction from metagenome assemblies. *PeerJ*. 2019.
